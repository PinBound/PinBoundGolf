YOUR ROUND, YOUR RHYTHM

Sound & play.

Two taps. Every shot.

  1. Tap the course to set your line. The aim sways; lock it at the right moment.
  2. Stop the moving meter near the gold band. “PIN” marks approach power; the full-shot pure band is just below 100.
  3. Finish on the green: gold ring adds one putt, pale ring two, outside adds three.

Space / Enter: lock or swing · Left / Right: adjust aim · 1–9: club · Escape: re-aim or close a panel. Water and out of bounds add one penalty stroke. This hole caps at 10 strokes.
